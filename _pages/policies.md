# Shipping Policy

## Processing Time

Orders are generally processed within **3–5 business days** after payment has been confirmed.

Made-to-order or custom products may require additional preparation time.

## Shipping Destinations

We ship internationally whenever possible.

Shipping availability may vary depending on the destination country and postal service.

## Delivery Times

Delivery times depend on your location and local postal services.

Estimated delivery dates are not guaranteed.

## Shipping Costs

Shipping fees are displayed during checkout.

Customers are responsible for any customs duties, VAT, import taxes, or other fees required by their country.

## Digital Products

Digital products are delivered electronically.

No physical shipment is made for digital downloads.

## Lost or Damaged Packages

If your order arrives damaged, or if there is a delivery issue, please contact us within **7 days** after delivery.

Please include photographs whenever possible.

# Return & Refund Policy

## Original Artwork

Returns are accepted within **14 days** of delivery only when:

- the artwork was damaged during shipping
- the item significantly differs from its description

Please contact us before returning any item.

## Custom Products

Custom-made or personalized items cannot be returned unless they are defective or incorrect.

## Digital Products

Because digital files are delivered immediately after purchase, all digital sales are final.

Refunds are not available once a digital product has been downloaded.

## Return Shipping

Unless the product is defective or shipped incorrectly, return shipping costs are the responsibility of the buyer.

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds will be issued using the original payment method.
